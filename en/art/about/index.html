Huize Frankendael is the only remaining 17th-century country estate of Amsterdam. The house carries a great deal of history and has witnessed many changes and stories in Amsterdam and the Netherlands: from republic to monarchy, from polder to city. It belongs to our cultural heritage. In Huize Frankendael and its garden, the Frankendael Foundation programmes contemporary visual art: a place where young artists in particular can develop and show work, and curators can make exhibitions.

Collection

Huize Frankendael has a permanent collection, “The Keys”. Commissioned by the Frankendael Foundation, several Dutch artists — including Bas Louter, Saskia Noor van Imhoff and Erik Odijk — made work in 2010 inspired by the rich history of the house and its residents: a journey through history with a contemporary eye. Odijk’s wall drawing is on permanent display in the upstairs garden room; the other works are regularly brought out of storage and shown anew. Parasite 22 by Dennis Feddersen (2011) can be seen every day in Restaurant Merkelbach (opens in a new tab).

Foundation

Stichting Huize Frankendael / Frankendael Foundation is registered with the Dutch tax authority as a public benefit organisation (ANBI), RSIN 820472657.

Its objective (from the 2010 statutes): to form a permanent basis for — and to promote, strengthen and make visible — the exchange between art, culture and science, the Dutch cultural and social field, and the (Dutch) public. The foundation pursues this by, among other things:

  • initiating and organising a programme of cultural activities in the broadest sense;
  • facilitating, promoting and realising visits to cultural events;
  • mediating contacts between people, cultural institutions and organisations;
  • creating opportunities for the presentation of cultural expression.

The Foundation follows the governance model of the Code Cultural Governance; board members receive no remuneration. The board consists of Nynke de Haan, Joram Kraaijeveld, Caroline Vos and Bodo Groen. Policy: spending subsidies, gifts and income usefully and meaningfully, in line with the objective and in consultation with the board.
