Contemporary art in an Amsterdam manorial estate

Frankendael Foundation welcomes the public to the estate and opens the house as an exhibition space for contemporary art. Inspired by the salon d’art, the Foundation makes art resonate throughout society by means of exhibitions, lively debates, networking and Art Dinners where artists, visitors, collectors and critics meet.

Huize Frankendael shows that contemporary art and the 17th-century national monument make an inspiring, exciting combination. There is currently no exhibition in the house; if you would like to organise one, email info@huizefrankendael.nl.
