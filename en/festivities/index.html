Unique location

As the last remaining country estate in Amsterdam, Huize Frankendael is one of the city’s top monuments. Its appeal goes far beyond its history: today you can celebrate your birthday, organise a dinner or get married here. All the salons and rooms throughout the house are open to you, for festivities of 10 to 300 people.

Private dining

The rooms provide a unique backdrop for a private dinner, catered by the kitchen of Restaurant Merkelbach (opens in a new tab). Both a sit-down dinner and a walking dinner are possible.

Parties and receptions

For birthdays or celebration parties you can use both the salons and the coach house, where we organise extensive receptions and parties with a DJ. Enjoy unforgettable moments in one of the rooms, carefree.

Weddings

Huize Frankendael is an official wedding venue. Weddings are celebrated in-house from beginning to end, and our team takes every care off your hands. The combination of indoors and outdoors makes this a unique place in Amsterdam. Read more about getting married at Huize Frankendael.

A thousand and one possibilities

A garden opera, a dinner for 300 in view of the house, or a “Hollandse Nieuwe” herring party? The possibilities at Huize Frankendael are endless; we embrace every idea, however playful.

Personal

Every party is personal, which is why we like to discuss your wishes over a cup of coffee.
