Parking

VOMAR multi-storey car park, diagonally opposite Huize Frankendael: secure and covered (± €6.00 per hour).

Paid street parking Mon–Sat 9 AM–9 PM: Hugo de Vrieslaan ± €3.90/h, Middenweg ± €5.00/h. Parking is free on Sundays.

Public transport

Huize Frankendael is located in the Watergraafsmeer area of Amsterdam-East and is easily reached by public transport:

  • From Amsterdam Muiderpoort station: tram or bus towards Watergraafsmeer, stop Hugo de Vrieslaan.
  • From Amsterdam Amstel station: city and regional buses along the Middenweg, stop Middenweg.
  • Plan your journey via 9292.nl (opens in a new tab) or GVB (opens in a new tab).

By car

From the direction of The Hague

Via the A4 towards Amsterdam. At Amsterdam join the A10 ring road south. Take the s113-Watergraafsmeer exit towards Diemen, carry straight on (follow s113 signs) and turn left at Middenweg/s113. Huize Frankendael is at No. 72 Middenweg after approx. 1.7 km, next to Restaurant De Kas.

From the direction of Amersfoort

Via the A1/E231 towards Amsterdam. At Amsterdam take the A10/E35 towards the Amsterdam ring (east). Take the s113-Watergraafsmeer exit towards Diemen, carry straight on (s113 signs) and turn right at Middenweg/s113. Huize Frankendael is at No. 72 Middenweg after approx. 1.7 km.

From the direction of Utrecht

Via the A2/E35 towards Amsterdam. Take the A10 towards Zaanstad. Take the s113-Watergraafsmeer exit towards Diemen, carry straight on (s113 signs) and turn left at Middenweg/s113. Huize Frankendael is at No. 72 Middenweg after approx. 1.7 km.

From the direction of Alkmaar

Via the A9 towards Amsterdam. Take the E19/A4 exit towards Amsterdam and join the A10. Take the s113-Watergraafsmeer exit towards Diemen, carry straight on (s113 signs) and turn left at Middenweg/s113. Huize Frankendael is at No. 72 Middenweg after approx. 1.7 km.
